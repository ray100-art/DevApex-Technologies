# Competitor Website Review

Reviewed 23 October 2026. Sites chosen from the top of the Clutch, The Manifest and GoodFirms
rankings for Nairobi, plus two larger firms as a quality benchmark.

| Company | Type | Headline / positioning |
|---|---|---|
| Segue Interactive | Nairobi product studio (ranked #1 on The Manifest) | "Build and launch digital products that move your business forward." |
| Intellspark | Nairobi web + marketing agency | "Transforming Ideas To Reality" / "Focused on Growing Brands Online" |
| Craft Silicon | Kenyan banking and fintech software vendor | Product-led, consultation first ("Conversations Beyond Demos") |
| Thoughtworks | Global consultancy (benchmark) | "We don't just do AI / We do AI that works" |
| Compwiz Creations | Nairobi agency | Site did not resolve at the time of review (itself a lesson) |

## What the best ones do

1. **Name the industries they serve.** Segue lists fintech, logistics, health and public sector. Craft Silicon is entirely about banks.
2. **Give a price anchor.** Segue: "Typical engagements start from $1,500 USD" plus a price calculator. This filters out bad leads and builds trust.
3. **Offer a low-risk first step.** Segue's "1-week free trial (40-hour sprint)", Craft Silicon's consultation call.
4. **Use measured results.** "85% reduction in order turnaround time" (Segue), even though it was unattributed.
5. **Get outside validation.** Clutch reviews, press mentions (Segue); analyst ratings, partner badges, Great Place to Work (Thoughtworks).
6. **Write short, confident copy.** Thoughtworks: one headline, one supporting line, one CTA per section.
7. **Offer more than one contact route.** WhatsApp, email and a form (Segue). Kenyan buyers expect WhatsApp.
8. **Show their own products.** Segue lists its own SaaS products, which proves it can ship.

## What the weak ones do (avoid all of these)

| Mistake seen | Where | DevApex rule |
|---|---|---|
| "We serve any type of business" | Intellspark | Lead with named industries and outcomes |
| Spelling errors ("Consultuncy", "develope") | Intellspark | Every page proof-read before launch |
| Links to `#`, placeholder Pricing/FAQ pages | Intellspark | No link ships without a real destination |
| Stats with no numbers, "Coffee Cups" counters | Intellspark | Only real, verifiable numbers, or none |
| Blog last updated 2017 | Intellspark | No blog until we can post monthly |
| Empty "Awards" section | Segue | No empty sections; hide until there is content |
| Unattributed results | Segue | Every result names the client (with permission) |
| No phone, address, or gated everything | Craft Silicon | Phone, WhatsApp, email and location on every page |
| Domain not resolving | Compwiz | Uptime monitoring from day one |

## Decisions for the DevApex website

- **Hero:** a specific promise, not a slogan. Name who we serve and what changes for them.
- **Industries section** with dedicated pages as soon as the niche is confirmed.
- **"From KES ..." price anchors** on every service page, plus clear packages.
- **Paid discovery workshop** as the low-risk first step (credited toward the build).
- **Case studies** written as challenge, solution, result, tech stack. ScoutBridge AI first.
- **4-step process with timelines** (Discover, Design, Build, Support).
- **WhatsApp, phone, email and quote form** reachable from every page.
- **Trust signals added as they are earned:** Clutch/Google reviews, client logos, certifications. Nothing invented.
- **Fast, mobile-first, accessible.** Lighthouse 90+ and real text, not text in images.
